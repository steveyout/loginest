Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Explore answers to the most frequently asked questions regarding customs clearance through Mombasa Port, transit times across the Northern Corridor, and documentation requirements.

What documents are required to clear cargo at Mombasa Port?

Standard imports require the Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Original Bill of Lading (or Sea Waybill), KEBS Certificate of Conformity (CoC), and the Import Declaration Form (IDF) registered on the KRA iCMS portal.

How fast can Loginest Logistics clear cargo to avoid demurrage?

With our KRA Authorized Economic Operator (AEO Green Lane) status and direct desk at Port of Mombasa Gate 18, compliant shipments with pre-filed documentation clear customs within 24 to 48 hours of vessel discharge.

What are typical transit times to Kampala and Kigali?

Direct trucking from Mombasa to Kampala typically takes 3 to 4 days, while Mombasa to Kigali takes 5 to 7 days, fully monitored via the East African Community Regional Electronic Cargo Tracking System (RECTS).

How do I get an immediate freight quotation?

You can request an instant quote online or message our freight coordinators directly on WhatsApp at +254 728 346457 with your cargo weight, volume (CBM), origin, and destination.
