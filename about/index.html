About Loginest Logistics Africa

With over 19 years of verified operational leadership, Loginest Logistics Africa provides comprehensive freight forwarding, customs brokerage, and multi-modal transit solutions throughout East & Central Africa and across 120+ global partner nations.

Headquarters: Bellway Business Park

Our central executive headquarters and logistics coordination command are located at Bellway Business Park, Mombasa Road, P.O Box 451-00521 Nairobi, Kenya. Equipped with real-time telematics, continuous transit monitoring, and bonded logistics management.

Port Operations: Mombasa Gate 18

Our dedicated harbor desk directly at Kenya Ports Authority Gate 18 in Kilindini Harbor handles immediate container destuffing, pre-arrival vessel clearance, and zero-demurrage cargo dispatch across the Northern Corridor.

Our Institutional Accreditations

Loginest Logistics maintains premier tier credentials with all governing transport and customs authorities:

  • KIFWA — Full Licensed Member of the Kenya International Freight and Warehousing Association.
  • FIATA — International Federation of Freight Forwarders Associations certified standard of trading.
