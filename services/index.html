Our Core Freight & Supply Chain Services

From full container loads arriving at Kilindini Port to emergency AOG aircraft parts flown into JKIA, Loginest Logistics Africa delivers audited logistics solutions tailored for multinational corporations, industrial manufacturers, and commercial importers.

1. Trucking & Commercial Distribution

Comprehensive domestic and cross-border transport connecting Mombasa, Nairobi, Kisumu, Eldoret, Kampala (Uganda), Kigali (Rwanda), and Juba (South Sudan). Our modern fleet features live GPS tracking, digital tamper-evident geo-fencing, and cargo insurance.

Northern Corridor Fleet • Cross-Border Bonded Transit

2. NVOCC & Cargo Consolidation

Weekly scheduled Less-than-Container Load (LCL) consolidation services from major ports in China, Europe, India, the UAE, and the USA. We offer dedicated Container Freight Station (CFS) destuffing, secure bonded warehousing, and unified cargo tracking.

Weekly LCL Consolidation • Bonded Warehousing

3. 45-Year AOG (Aircraft On Ground) Aviation Logistics

Mission-critical emergency logistics for commercial airlines, MROs, and charter operators. Our 24/7 aviation desk at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) provides 60-minute customs clearance for engines, avionics, and structural components.

24/7 JKIA Airfield Clearance • 60-Minute Tarmac Handover

4. Project Logistics & Heavy Rigging

Turnkey solutions for out-of-gauge (OOG) and heavy-lift equipment serving geothermal, wind energy, road construction, and mining infrastructure projects throughout East Africa with full route reconnaissance and specialized multi-axle trailers.

Multi-Axle Hydraulic Trailers • Turnkey Engineering Escorts

5. International Freight Forwarding (120+ Countries)

Global multi-modal freight covering air, ocean (FCL/LCL), and multimodal rail/road connections with premier shipping lines and airlines. Direct service contracts provide competitive freight rates, space guarantees, and end-to-end visibility.

WCA Verified Network • 120+ Partner Nations

6. Mombasa Port Customs Clearance

Zero-demurrage customs clearance utilizing KRA AEO Green Lane status and direct integration with Kenya Ports Authority Gate 18. Pre-clearance documentation, KEBS Certificate of Conformity compliance, and automated manifest filings.

KPA Gate 18 Office • KRA AEO Green Lane Priority
